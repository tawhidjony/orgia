About the Event

Dhaka Fashion Week 2026 is the premier fashion event in Bangladesh, showcasing the country's most talented designers on an international runway. This year's theme, "Heritage Reimagined," celebrates the fusion of traditional Bangladeshi craftsmanship with contemporary global aesthetics.

Over three days, attendees will experience runway shows, designer meet-and-greets, textile exhibitions, and networking sessions with industry leaders from across South Asia and beyond.
