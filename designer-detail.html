Biography

Rima Sultana is one of Bangladesh's most celebrated contemporary fashion designers, known for seamlessly blending traditional Bengali motifs with modern silhouettes. With over 15 years of experience, she has showcased her collections at Dhaka Fashion Week and international platforms.

Her design philosophy centers on honoring heritage while embracing innovation. Each piece tells a story of Bangladeshi craftsmanship, from hand-woven jamdani to intricate embroidery techniques passed down through generations.

Signature Style

Contemporary fusion with traditional motifs — elegant draping, rich textures, and a palette inspired by Bengal's natural landscapes. Rima's work is characterized by fluid lines, artisanal details, and a commitment to wearable luxury.

Craftsmanship

Every garment is crafted in collaboration with master artisans across Dhaka and Narayanganj. Rima works directly with jamdani weavers, nakshi kantha embroiderers, and block printers to ensure authentic techniques are preserved and elevated.

Sustainability

Rima is committed to ethical production, using natural dyes, organic cotton, and supporting fair wages for artisan communities. Her atelier operates with minimal waste and prioritizes timeless pieces over fast fashion.
